October 7, 2022. Washington, D.C.
Washington builds a wall across the Pacific…
[deadpan] It's a wall made of paperwork.
In October 2022, export controls block top AI chips from going to China.
The rule works like a highway speed limit.
Is your chip too fast?

You need a federal license.
Shipping to Beijing?
[sarcastic] Good luck getting that stamp.
So how does Nvidia respond?
Engineers design slightly slower chips — just for China.
Problem solved?

[dry] Not for long.

In October 2023, Washington lowers the speed limit again.
Those China-only chips get banned too.
So Nvidia designs an even SLOWER chip: the H20.
Think of a Ferrari… with a lawnmower engine.

[small child voice, whiny] “Why am I so slow?”
[small child voice, flat and bossy] “You're the LEGAL model.”

Then, in April 2025, Washington restricts the H20 as well.
Nvidia takes a 5.5-billion-dollar charge on unsold inventory.
[dry] They baked the cake, and Uncle Sam cancelled the party.

[flat bureaucrat voice] “This chip is too fast for China.”
[smooth salesman voice, upbeat] “No problem! We made a slower one.”
[flat bureaucrat voice] “That one is also too fast.”
[smooth salesman voice, upbeat] “So we made an even slower one!”
[flat bureaucrat voice] “Still… too fast.”
[smooth salesman voice, suddenly nervous] “What if I just ship the empty box?”
[flat bureaucrat voice, bored] “Fill out form seven.”

Every physical door to China looks locked tight.
Why?
Because the rules watch where physical boxes go…
[lower, conspiratorial] Not where the math goes.
