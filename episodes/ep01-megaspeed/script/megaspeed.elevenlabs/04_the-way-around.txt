2024. Southeast Asia.
Border guards are GREAT at stopping cardboard boxes.
[deadpan] They are terrible at stopping Wi-Fi.
Imagine your mom bans a PlayStation from your bedroom.
How do you beat the ban?
Your neighbor buys ten PlayStations instead.

He keeps every console inside his OWN house.
Then he runs a long controller cable through your window.
You don't move the machine…
[mischievously] You just move the game.

In Silicon Valley, that's called cloud computing.
Shipping an H100 chip to China requires a U.S. license.
Putting it in a data center in Malaysia?
Generally… legal.
Letting overseas clients rent that power over the internet?
[deadpan] Also legal.

[flat bureaucrat voice] “Did any of these chips go to China?”
[laid-back, casual] “Nope. The servers stay riiight here in Malaysia.”
[flat bureaucrat voice] “Then what leaves the building?”
[laid-back, casual] “Just the login… and the answers.”
[flat bureaucrat voice] “Is there a form for answers?”
[laid-back, casual] “You tell me, man.”
[flat bureaucrat voice, bored] “…Fill out form seven.”

Lawmakers have a name for this gap.
The cloud loophole.

Remember the neighbor…

[lower, conspiratorial] Because in Singapore, a tiny firm is about to test that cable.
